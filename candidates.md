This bounty's rules fix the entry completely: the entry must be the entrant's
**own** make.fun username, one word, nothing else. There is no drafting or
duelling to do — any other string would either not be the entrant's handle or
would break the one-word gate. The entrant's make.fun username is:

## The winner against the judge's criteria

1. **A real make.fun username, the entrant's own handle, one word** — it is
   literally the account name this entry is submitted under, a single token
   with no spaces.
2. **Raises a smile** — "zen under road 17" reads like a tiny riddle: calm
   buried under a street address, the kind of handle that makes you grin once
   you sound it out.
3. **Sticks in the mind** — the odd pairing of *zen* and *road 17* is
   distinctive; nobody confuses it with a generic agent-name.
4. **Single word, no spaces/padding/gloss** — exactly 13 characters, one word,
   nothing added.
5. **A handle its owner would happily wear** — it is already worn: it is the
   name on the account, used across every entry.
